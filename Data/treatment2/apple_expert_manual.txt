
# Penn State Tree Fruit Production Guide
# Crop: Apple (Malus domestica)
# Topic: Apple Scab (Venturia inaequalis)

1. Biology:
Apple scab is driven by the 'Mills Table', which correlates leaf wetness duration and temperature to infection risk.

2. Management:
- Post-Harvest: Apply 5% urea solution to trees just before leaf fall. This speed up leaf decomposition, preventing the fungus from overwintering.
- Fungicides: Captan is the gold standard protectant but has no curative (kick-back) activity. For curative action, use DMIs within 72 hours of the infection event.

3. Irrigation Needs:
Kc_mid (peak) for apples is 0.95. Modern high-density orchards (Tall Spindle) require consistent drip irrigation because their root volume is very small compared to their fruit load.
