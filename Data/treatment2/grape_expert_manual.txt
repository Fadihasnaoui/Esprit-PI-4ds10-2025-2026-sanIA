
# UC IPM - Viticulture Extension Guidelines
# Crop: Grape (Vitis vinifera)
# Topic: Black Rot Management

1. Cultural Controls:
Pruning is the most effective way to manage Black Rot. Removing 'mummies' (shriveled infected berries) reduces up to 90% of the primary inoculum for the next spring.

2. Professional Treatment:
- Fungicide Timing: The most critical period for spraying is from 'immediate pre-bloom' through 4 weeks post-bloom.
- Resistance Management: Rotate DMIs (e.g., Myclobutanil) with Strobilurins.
- Organic Options: Copper fungicides are effective but can cause 'stunting' in cold, wet springs.

3. Irrigation & Quality:
According to FAO-56, grapes have low water demand early in the season (Kc 0.3). 'Regulated Deficit Irrigation' (RDI) is often used post-veraison to increase sugar content and polyphenols.
